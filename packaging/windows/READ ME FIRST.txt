T.N.W.R. - The Naggy Wife Reminder  (Windows beta)
==================================================

A reminder that gets louder and louder until you PROVE the task is done.


INSTALL (about 1 minute)
------------------------
1. Right-click the downloaded .zip file -> "Extract All..." -> Extract.
   (Don't run it from inside the zip.)
2. In the extracted folder, double-click  "Install TNWR".
3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC":
   click "More info", then "Run anyway". (The beta isn't signed yet.)
4. A black window shows the progress, then T.N.W.R. opens. Press any key
   to close the black window.

No admin rights needed. T.N.W.R. is added to the Start menu and your
desktop, and starts by itself (hidden in the tray) when you sign in.


USING IT
--------
- "New reminder" (bottom right): name it, pick a time, choose how
  annoying it gets and how you have to prove it's done.
- Try it: on any reminder, the three dots -> "Test: ring in 5 seconds".
- When it rings, the alarm fills the screen and gets louder (it turns up
  your PC's volume and turns it back down afterwards). Solve the proof to
  stop it.
- Closing the window just hides it in the tray (near the clock). Reminders
  keep working. Right-click the tray icon -> Quit to exit completely.
- On a Teams/Zoom/Discord call (mic or camera in use), a ringing alarm
  waits until the call is over.
- Settings (gear icon, top right): light/dark mode, call pausing, and
  "Connect Alexa".


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG (beta!)
-------------------------------
If an alarm won't stop because of a glitch, hold the "Hold 10 s to cancel
without proof" button on the alarm screen for 10 seconds, then confirm.

Please tell whoever sent you T.N.W.R. what happened: what you did, what you expected, and
what it did instead. A screenshot helps (Windows key + Shift + S).


UPDATE / REMOVE
---------------
- Update: extract the new zip and run "Install TNWR" again. Your reminders
  are kept.
- Remove: open  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\TNWR  (paste that into the File
  Explorer address bar) and double-click "Uninstall TNWR". Your reminders
  are kept in %APPDATA%\com.nagalarm in case you reinstall; delete that
  folder too if you want them gone.
